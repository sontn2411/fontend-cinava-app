# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Viewers looking to stream or watch video content online.

## Product Purpose

A seamless video streaming platform for movies and shows.

## Positioning

A fast, minimalist video streaming experience with focus on playback quality.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, immersive, premium (like Netflix or Apple TV).

## Anti-references

Cluttered interfaces with too many menus (avoid traditional cable TV UIs).

## Design Principles

- Focus on the content, ensuring the UI gets out of the way of the video.
- Prioritize cinematic immersion using dark, premium aesthetics that make the content pop.
- Deliver frictionless playback with fast, responsive, and minimalist interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific requirements yet. Standard web practices apply.
